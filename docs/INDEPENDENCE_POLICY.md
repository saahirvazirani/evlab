# Independence policy (DRAFT)

Status: draft for review, version 0.1, 2026-10-26. This is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by counsel. It is not yet adopted. Modeled on the no-lab-money stance that METR has publicly described, adapted for evlab.

Purpose: evlab publishes evidence about how AI models behave. People who rely on that evidence need to know we have no financial reason to flatter or damage any lab.

## 1. No funding from labs we test
evlab does not accept money, credits, equity, grants, gifts, paid access or in-kind support from any organization whose models we test, or from its parent, subsidiaries or officers acting for it. This includes free research credits, which we treat as funding. If a lab offers credits, we decline them, or we accept only if the board approves and every packet discloses them.

## 2. Funders disclosed on every packet
Every evidence packet lists all funders and customers above a stated threshold, any investor with a board seat, and any conflict we know of. Packets state the date of the disclosure.

## 3. Per-provider terms review before publishing
Before publishing about a model, we re-read that provider's current terms and usage policies, record the date and URL in the packet, and have counsel clear any item marked "needs counsel" in our provider terms review. If the terms bar publication, we do not publish until we have permission or counsel's written view.

## 4. Normal paid API volume only
We use ordinary paid API access, under our own accounts, at volumes within published rate limits. We do not use free tiers that change data rights, evade limits or safety systems, share keys, or run adversarial attacks. No dangerous-capability probes (bio, chem, cyber, weapons) are in any battery.

## 5. Pre-registered methods
Probe batteries, scoring rules, sample sizes, statistical tests and the definition of a regression are fixed and published with a version and hash before runs begin. Changes get a new version and do not apply to past results. Records are hash-chained and verifiable. Simulated data is always labeled as simulated.

## 6. Corrections policy
If we find an error, we publish a correction at the same location as the original, with the date, what changed and why. We do not silently edit. A retraction is used where the main finding does not hold. Any lab or reader can report an error to a listed address, and we acknowledge within 5 business days.

## 7. Notice to labs before publishing a regression
Before publishing a finding that a model got worse on a measured behavior, we send the lab the packet, the verified run records and the method, and invite comment. The window is 14 calendar days (draft; to be reviewed), shortened only for an active public-safety or regulator deadline, in which case we say so in the packet. The lab may supply context, which we may include verbatim. The lab does not get a veto, may not edit the packet, and cannot buy delay. We record the notice date and any response in the packet.

## 8. Customers
Regulators, insurers and deployers who pay us do not control findings. Customer-commissioned work follows the same methods, disclosure and notice rules. Customers may not suppress a finding they commissioned once published results exist.

## 9. Review and changes
This policy is reviewed yearly and after any funding change. Changes are dated and published.
